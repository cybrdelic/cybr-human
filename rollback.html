This candidate couples a regional adipose volume and thin dermal solid to rest-dihedral skin bending. Fat growth changes adipose volume and mass; gravity and skin bending act during each solve. The detailed skin follows it on the GPU. On-demand controls solve static equilibrium; Run simulation uses fixed-time dynamics. Depths and material values are estimates. A local forehead membrane/foundation FEM adds mechanical wrinkle detail from solved strain. Its coupling is one-way; full-face shell mechanics remain pending. Rest anatomy shares the skin's native coordinate frame; it is not an activated muscle or jaw simulation.

Tissue layers and limitations Iterative dynamic FEM with energy and positive-volume checks. The simulation mesh is repaired to maintain positive cells and finite layer thickness. Thin features still use approximate tissue layers. The fixed inner substrate is an approximation. Bone/cartilage contact uses fixed nearest-triangle tangents; global moving contact, fascia, activated muscles and self-contact are pending.
